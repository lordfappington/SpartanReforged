# Reforged Main Menu Art Direction

## North star

> Spartan: Total Warrior as remembered through modern hardware.

The Reforged menu must be recognizably descended from the original `main_start`, not a generic contemporary launcher. Modern fidelity removes PS2 resolution and presentation constraints while the original hierarchy, floating navigation, Spartan identity, and semantic behavior remain legible underneath.

## Approved direction

- Large faithful SPARTAN / TOTAL WARRIOR identity in the upper-left.
- Floating vertical navigation on the left; no large rectangular menu buttons.
- Dark blue/black atmospheric base with restrained aged-gold highlights.
- Ancient Greek/Roman ornamental language and thin top/bottom friezes.
- Low visual noise behind the logo, navigation, context, and prompts.
- Distant battlefield/city depth through the centre, with richer environmental interest on the right.
- Optional right-side foreground helmet, shield, banner, spears, rock, or debris composition, kept separate from UI.
- Warm selected state with a small spear/weapon-like marker.
- Modern scalable typography that retains classical character and supports localization.
- Recognizable metallic PlayStation-style prompt housings, with platform-independent semantic actions beneath them.
- Smoke, mist, embers, selected glow, and logo glint used subtly rather than as spectacle.
- Native 16:9 composition with environment-only 21:9/32:9 extension.

## Restraint rules

- Do not become a bright generic fantasy landscape.
- Do not turn navigation into boxed cards or a mobile/launcher layout.
- Do not bake any dynamic UI into environmental art.
- Do not replace the logo with a newly invented brand.
- Do not upscale or paint over preservation assets as the production source.
- Do not let foreground imagery compete with text.
- Do not treat concept or wireframe imagery as canonical final art.
- Do not use particles as a substitute for composition.

## Palette and material language

The working palette is semantic, not approved final colour grading: blue-black environment, cool neutral text, aged gold selection/ornament, and sparse warm firelight. Surfaces may suggest weathered bronze, iron, carved stone, ash, cloth, and smoke. Gold should read as controlled light/material response rather than saturated yellow.

## Relationship to the original

The original supplies behavioral truth: menu order, selected state, contextual meaning, lock rule, prompts, logo priority, floating navigation, atmospheric background, and ornamental framing. Reforged changes rendering, resolution, layout adaptability, typography, motion polish, and asset fidelity. Original presentation remains available as an independent future view and is never overwritten.

## Human approval gates

The supplied high-resolution raster logo is human-approved and integrated. Typeface, prompt glyphs, marker, padlock, and motion language still require design review.

The main-menu background plate is now also human-approved. The supplied 1280×720 RGB JPEG is archived and used byte-for-byte; runtime presentation scales the complete 16:9 plate into the central composition without cropping. Its ornamental top and bottom bands are baked into the approved artwork, so the procedural placeholder bands are suppressed whenever this plate is active. Logo, navigation, selection, context, padlock, and controller prompts remain independent UI layers.

## Reforged art pipeline principle

Original assets are evidence of artistic intent, not mandatory modern rendering constraints.

1. Recover original geometry, material, texture, and animation evidence.
2. Determine the intended visual design.
3. Produce modern concept/reference artwork where appropriate.
4. Human-review the artistic direction.
5. Only after approval, convert and integrate it into the runtime pipeline.
6. Never allow automated reconstruction to override approved art direction.

This applies to future characters, armour, weapons, environments, foliage, architecture, props, and UI. Preservation sources and interpretations remain immutable alongside Reforged production work.
